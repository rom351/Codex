50 Midjourney Prompts: Isometric 3D App Icons
============================================================

HOW TO USE
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Midjourney and start a new image.
2. Copy any prompt from this pack and paste it in full, including the parameters at the end.
3. Press Enter. Midjourney returns a set of icon variations.
4. Pick your favorite, upscale it, and download the result.

TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Change the colors: replace the palette words (for example "blue and white" with "black and gold").
- Change the object: replace the part after "app icon," with anything you like, such as "a rocket" or "a camera".
- Change the material: swap "glossy plastic" for "matte clay", "frosted glass" or "brushed metal".
- Keep the icon free of text: add --no text at the end.
- Parameters: --ar 1:1 keeps the icon square, --v 7 selects the model version. Adjust --v if you use another version.
